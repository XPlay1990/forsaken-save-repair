    How to use it

    1. Close the game & back up

      Copy your entire ForsakenKingdom folder somewhere safe before you begin.

    2. Choose folder or ZIP

      Select the whole campaign folder. To use a ZIP, right-click the folder in File Explorer and choose Compress to ZIP file or Send to → Compressed (zipped) folder.

    3. Repair & download

      Check the result, then download the repaired ZIP. Your saves stay on your computer throughout.

    4. Restore the bundle

      Extract the ZIP, then copy its contents into your original ForsakenKingdom folder. Include Blizzard and keep the directory structure. Companion saves and cache files may replace existing files.

    5. Load a _repaired save

      Open Warcraft III, load your repaired checkpoint, and try travelling between locations. Confirm your progress, then save again.

    Use the complete folder: each visited location can have its own companion save. This tool repairs patch compatibility; some saves or gameplay changes may still be unsupported.
