      AFTER THE DOWNLOAD

      Put the bundle back together.

      1. Close Warcraft III. Make a separate backup of your existing campaign folder.
      2. Extract the repaired ZIP. Copy its campaign files into the same ForsakenKingdom folder, including the repaired companion files inside Blizzard. Keep the directory structure.
      3. Load, travel, save. Check both destinations and return to Undercity. Confirm your progress, then save under a new name.
      Where is the campaign folder?

      Windows: Documents\Warcraft III\BattleNet\<account-number>\Campaigns\ForsakenKingdom

      The page cannot change this folder. It gives you a new ZIP to restore yourself.
